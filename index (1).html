Sec. 1

About

With a focused practice across criminal defence, family matters, negotiable instruments (cheque dishonour) cases, and electricity-related disputes, this office provides practical, client-first legal guidance to individuals and families across Jamnagar and the surrounding region.

Every matter — whether a contested hearing or a routine consultation — is handled with the same attention to detail, clear communication, and honest assessment of where a case stands and what to expect next.

Advocate Yajuvendrasinh Parmar has been practicing since February 2020 with R R Raval & Associates in Jamnagar, focusing on family law, negotiable instruments (cheque dishonour), and criminal legal matters. He holds an LL.B. from Harivandna College and a B.Sc. in Mathematics from Saurashtra University.

Before turning to law, he spent over a decade in technical and field roles — including chemist positions handling water and fuel analysis for industrial clients, disaster relief coordination after the Kashmir earthquake and Tamil Nadu tsunami, and financial advisory work in stocks, insurance, and mutual funds. This background brings a grounded, detail-oriented approach to documentation, technical matters, and working directly with people through difficult situations.
